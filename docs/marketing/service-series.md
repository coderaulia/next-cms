## Part 1 — Value proposition by customer type

Each row covers one type of potential customer: what they struggle with, which offers fit, and the promise that matters to them.

| Customer type | What they struggle with | Best-fit offers | Value proposition (what we promise) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Small business / UMKM owner** | No time, limited budget, looks less credible than bigger competitors | Templates, Website Development (Startup), Official Business Email | *Look established from day one.* A professional site and company email, live fast, that you can update yourself. |
| **Retail & fashion brand** | Marketplace fees, WhatsApp orders piling up, no customer data | Secure Online Shops, Mobile Business App, Atelier | *Own your sales channel.* A secure store with payments, stock sync, and WhatsApp built in, and the customers stay yours. |
| **Exporter / B2B manufacturer** | International buyers judge credibility online, and catalogs are hard to share | Website Development, Secure Online Shops (B2B), Official Business Email | *Win buyer trust before the first call.* An export-ready showcase and domain email that pass the credibility check. |
| **Service business** (clinic, law firm, consultant, travel) | Enquiries scattered, and the site doesn't explain services clearly | Website Development, Templates, Atelier | *Turn visitors into bookings.* Clear service pages with one next action on every page. |
| **Growing company, ops/finance team** | Spreadsheet chaos, approvals in chat, slow reports | Custom Business Tools | *One source of truth.* Your process in a tool with an audit trail and reports in minutes. |
| **HR team** | KPI spreadsheets, guesswork TNA, manual HR letters | Vanaila HRIS, Psikotest | *HR on one record.* KPIs, competencies, and letters connected, with one approval workflow and one audit trail. |
| **Sales team / sales manager** | Lost leads, no pipeline visibility, can't coach with data | Flowraze CRM, Custom Business Tools | *See what drives revenue.* Every lead and deal in one visual pipeline. |
| **Recruiter / psychology bureau / researcher** | Paper tests, manual scoring, inconsistent reports | Psikotest, Custom Business Tools | *Assessments that run themselves.* Online delivery, automatic scoring, and expert interpretation. |
| **Startup / product founder** | Need an MVP on iOS and Android without two teams | Mobile Business App, Atelier (UI & Product) | *Launch on both stores from one codebase.* Store approval is handled for you. |
| **Brand / marketing lead** | Inconsistent brand, generic creative | Vanaila Atelier, Website Development | *A brand that scales.* An identity system from business card to billboard, with design your developers can build. |

**The overall promise:** *High-performance websites, custom software, and digital systems, built around how your business actually works.*

**Why Vanaila (shared proof points)**
- Real portfolio across research, education, ecommerce/export, HR, manufacturing, psychology, and tourism
- We build our own products (HRIS, Flowraze, Psikotest), so we run what we build
- Design and engineering in one team (Atelier + dev)
- We'll tell you when you *don't* need custom work (templates, off-the-shelf options)
