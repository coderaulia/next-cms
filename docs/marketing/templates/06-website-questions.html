Before you build

3 questions to answer
before you build
a website.

1
What should a visitor do next?

Call, WhatsApp, book, buy? One clear action beats ten nice pages.

2
Who updates it after launch?

If every text change needs a developer, the site goes stale in 3 months.

3
How will you know it's working?

Decide your numbers — leads, calls, sales — before the first design.

TipWrite the answers down.
Share them with whoever builds your site.
