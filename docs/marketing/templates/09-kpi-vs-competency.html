KPI vs Competency.
Same thing? No.

The result

KPI

"What did they achieve?"
  • Measurable targets
  • Reviewed monthly / quarterly
  • Numbers, % attainment
e.g. Close 20 deals per quarter
The capability

Competency

"How do they work?"
  • Skills & behaviours per role
  • Scored against a rubric
  • Feeds training plans (TNA)
e.g. Negotiation, level 3 of 5
How to read them together
Missed KPI + low competency→ train
Missed KPI + high competency→ fix the process
Hit KPI + low competency→ coach before promoting
