Why is my website
so slow?

4 common culprits, and what to ask your developer.

01
Huge images

Photos uploaded straight from a camera or phone, several MB each.

FIXCompress & serve WebP / AVIF.
02
Too many plugins

Every add-on loads its own scripts — even on pages that don't use it.

FIXAudit & remove what you don't need.
03
Overloaded hosting

Cheap shared servers slow down when neighbours get busy.

FIXRight-size hosting + use a CDN.
04
No caching

The server rebuilds every page from scratch for every visitor.

FIXTurn on page & browser caching.
