## Post 5 — Your email address is your sales pitch (`output/05-business-email.png`)

**Instagram / Facebook**

Before a client reads your offer, they've already read your email address. 👀

tokobudi.official88@gmail.com vs hello@tokobudi.com. The second one is trusted before anyone opens the email.

A domain email also comes with 3 quiet protectors:
🔐 SPF: which servers may send as you
✍️ DKIM: a signature that proves the email is really from you
🛡️ DMARC: blocks look-alike phishing using your name

60-second check: open any email you sent in Gmail → ⋮ → "Show original" → look for PASS on all three.

Small detail, big trust. Need help setting it up? We do this every week.

#EmailBisnis #BusinessEmail #GoogleWorkspace #CyberSecurity #BrandTrust #VanailaDigital

**LinkedIn**

Your email domain is the first trust signal in every B2B conversation, and people often skip it.

Beyond looking professional, a properly configured domain gives you:
• SPF: authorises which servers can send as your company
• DKIM: cryptographically signs each message
• DMARC: tells inboxes to reject spoofed mail using your domain

Try it now: Gmail → open a sent email → "Show original". If you don't see PASS on all three, your emails may be landing in spam, and anyone could impersonate you.

## Post 6 — 3 questions before you build a website (`output/06-website-questions.png`)

**Instagram / Facebook**

Most website projects go off track before the first design is made. 🧭

Answer these 3 first:
1️⃣ What should a visitor do next? (Call, WhatsApp, book, buy?)
2️⃣ Who updates the site after launch?
3️⃣ How will you know it's working? (Leads, calls, sales)

Write the answers down and hand them to whoever builds your site, whether that's us or someone else. It'll save weeks. 🙌

Save this, and send it to a founder friend who's planning a new site.

#TipsWebsite #WebsiteBisnis #WebDevelopment #SmallBusinessTips #UMKMGoDigital #VanailaDigital

**LinkedIn**

The best website briefs we get aren't long. They answer three questions:

1. What is the single next action a visitor should take?
2. Who owns content updates after launch, and do they need a developer to do it?
3. Which numbers define success: leads, calls, bookings, or sales?

Get those right and design, copy, and tech decisions get much easier. Save this for your next website project.
