Our Story

Three Decades of
Beauty Innovation

What began as a small family workshop in Surabaya in 1994 has grown into one of Indonesia's most trusted personal care brands — with 27 products reaching over 10,000 retail points across Sumatera, Java, and Bali.

Natural Ingredients
We source the finest botanicals from Indonesian farms, crafting formulas that honor traditional beauty wisdom.
Science-Backed Care
Every product undergoes rigorous testing in our R&D lab, ensuring safety and efficacy you can trust.
Accessible Beauty
Premium quality at fair prices — because everyone deserves to feel confident in their own skin.

Sustainability

Beauty That Cares

We believe in creating products that are kind to you and the planet.

Clean Ingredients
Every product is free from parabens, sulfates, and harmful chemicals. We source natural botanicals from local Indonesian farms committed to sustainable agriculture.
Eco Packaging
80% of our packaging is made from recycled or biodegradable materials. We're on track to reach 100% sustainable packaging by 2028.
Community Impact
We partner with women-led cooperatives across Java and Bali, empowering 500+ local families through fair-trade ingredient sourcing programs.
