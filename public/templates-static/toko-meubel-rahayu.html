A Legacy of Sunter Craftsmanship

For over three decades, Toko Meubel Rahayu has been a cornerstone of the Sunter community. What started as a small family workshop has evolved into Jakarta's premier destination for furniture that balances heritage techniques with modern lifestyle needs.

We believe that furniture is an investment in your home's story. That's why we prioritize durability and timeless aesthetics over fleeting trends. Every joint, finish, and fabric selection is vetted by our master artisans who have been with us since the beginning.
